National Standards, Laws & Public Review Portal
A centralized digital platform where standards, laws, regulations, policies, and draft documents are published in structured stages, and citizens/stakeholders can review, comment, track, and receive transparent responses through an AI-assisted ticketing and workflow engine.
A good working name could be:
Qanoon & Standards Review Portal (QSRP)
or
Pakistan Digital Standards & Public Review Portal
or
e-Consult Pakistan
1) Core purpose
This portal should solve 5 major problems:
Draft laws/standards are hard to discover
Public review is mostly manual and non-transparent
Feedback disappears into departments with no tracking
Ministries delay responses
Citizens do not know whether feedback was accepted, rejected, or still under review
So the platform should make the whole lifecycle visible:
Drafting → Public Review → Feedback Submission → Ticket Issuance → Department Review → Approval/Denial → Response Published → Final Version Issued
2) Main document modes
Every item on the portal should have a clear status badge:
A. Draft Mode
Internal only
Accessible to authorized ministry / department / PDA users
Version control enabled
AI can help summarize changes
B. Public Review Mode
Public can read online
Public can download PDF / DOC
Public can submit feedback
Each feedback creates a traceable ticket
Submitter can track progress live
C. Published Mode
Final approved law / regulation / standard / policy
Public archive
Searchable by sector, ministry, date, version, and tags
Previous versions preserved for audit

15) Best short concept note for chairperson
You can share this:
Sir, this second product can be a national portal for publishing standards, laws, regulations, and draft policies in structured modes such as Draft, Public Review, and Published. During Public Review, citizens and stakeholders can log in via Google/email, read or download the document, and submit clause-wise feedback. Every feedback becomes a ticket with AI-based classification, routing, tracking, SLA, escalation, and transparent status updates. Ministries/departments will respond within defined timelines, while submitters can see whether feedback is approved, partially accepted, or denied with reasons. The system will work like a JIRA-style governance workflow with public transparency and executive dashboards.
